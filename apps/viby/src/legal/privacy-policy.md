# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Viby ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide to Us

**Account Information**: When you authenticate with Spotify or Deezer, we collect:
- Your username and display name
- Profile picture/avatar
- Email address (if provided by the streaming service)
- User ID from the streaming service

**Profile Information**: You may provide additional information such as:
- Favorite artists and music preferences
- Bio or profile description
- Notification preferences
- Privacy settings

**User Content**: We collect content you create or share through the App:
- Chat messages with matched users
- Game session data and scores
- Match history and interactions

### Information Collected Automatically

**Music Listening Data**: With your permission, we collect:
- Currently playing track information (title, artist, album)
- Listening history and patterns
- Music taste preferences and statistics
- Top artists, tracks, and genres

**Usage Data**: We automatically collect:
- App usage patterns and feature interactions
- Screen views and navigation paths
- Session duration and frequency
- Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers)
- IP address and general location (city/country level)

**Performance Data**: We collect technical information to improve the App:
- Crash reports and error logs
- API response times and latency metrics
- WebSocket connection quality
- App performance metrics

### Information from Third Parties

**Streaming Service Data**: We receive information from Spotify or Deezer when you connect your account:
- Your public profile information
- Currently playing track data
- Your top artists and tracks
- Playlists and listening history (with your permission)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

**Provide Core Services**:
- Enable realtime discovery of users listening to the same music
- Match you with other users based on shared listening
- Facilitate game sessions and chat conversations
- Display your profile and music statistics

**Improve User Experience**:
- Personalize content and recommendations
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
- Optimize app performance and reliability
- Develop new features and functionality

**Communication**:
- Send push notifications for matches and messages
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Send important updates about the App or policy changes

**Safety and Security**:
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect the rights and safety of our users

**Analytics and Research**:
- Understand how users interact with the App
- Measure feature adoption and engagement
- Conduct research to improve our services
- Generate aggregated, anonymized statistics

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

**With Other Users**: Your profile information, currently playing track, and match history are visible to other users of the App as part of the core functionality.

**With Service Providers**: We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:
- Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud)
- Analytics services (Amplitude, Mixpanel)
- Error tracking services (Sentry)
- Push notification services (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification Service)

**With Streaming Services**: We share minimal information with Spotify or Deezer as required to maintain your connected account and access listening data.

**For Legal Reasons**: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Viby, our users, or others.

**Business Transfers**: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

**With Your Consent**: We may share information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

- **Account Data**: Retained until you delete your account
- **Chat Messages**: Retained for 90 days after the conversation ends
- **Match History**: Retained for 1 year
- **Analytics Data**: Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained indefinitely
- **Crash Reports**: Retained for 90 days

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@viby.app.

## Your Rights and Choices

**Access and Update**: You can access and update your profile information through the App's settings.

**Delete Account**: You can request account deletion by contacting us at privacy@viby.app. We will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

**Opt-Out of Communications**: You can disable push notifications through your device settings or the App's notification preferences.

**Disconnect Streaming Service**: You can disconnect your Spotify or Deezer account at any time through the App's settings.

**Data Portability**: You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at privacy@viby.app.

**Do Not Track**: We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- Secure token storage using platform-specific secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore)
- Certificate pinning for API communications
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Access controls and authentication for internal systems

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## Children's Privacy
